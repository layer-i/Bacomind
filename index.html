The science in full and references

Neurotransmitter modulation (the chemical messengers)

Acetylcholine boost: Bacosides inhibit acetylcholinesterase, an enzyme that breaks down acetylcholine. By keeping acetylcholine active in the brain longer, bacopa helps improve memory retention, focus, and overall cognitive processing speed.* Serotonin and GABA balance: Bacosides help regulate mood and ease anxiety by supporting the brain’s calming neurotransmitters.*

Neuroprotection & antioxidant defence

Free radical neutralisation: Brain cells are highly vulnerable to oxidative stress. Bacosides act as powerful antioxidants, neutralising free radicals and preventing lipid peroxidation (damage to fat molecules in brain cells).[1]* Axon and dendrite repair: Preclinical studies suggest bacosides actively help repair damaged neurons and promote the growth of dendritic branches, the microscopic “arms” brain cells use to communicate with one another. This forms the biological basis for improved learning and memory consolidation.*
